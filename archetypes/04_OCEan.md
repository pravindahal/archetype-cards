### OCEan - The Mastermind

- **Gender Probability:** Male (Both low Agreeableness and low Neuroticism heavily lean male statistically, resulting in a highly stoic, competitive, and dominant profile)
- **Light Attributes:** Boldly visionary, highly disciplined and strategic, exceptionally outgoing and dominant, fiercely logical in decision-making, and completely unflappable under extreme pressure.
- **Shadow Attributes:** Can be ruthlessly competitive, rigidly controlling, entirely dismissive of others' emotions, and dangerously detached from the human impact of their ambitions.
- **Visual Concept:** A cold, intensely focused male figure standing atop a modern glass skyscraper at night, overlooking a sprawling, illuminated city. He wears a sharp, structured suit, exuding an aura of unbreakable calm, dominant intellect, and ruthless efficiency.
