### ocean - The Outlaw

- **Gender Probability:** Male (The combination of low Openness, low Agreeableness, and low Neuroticism statistically leans male, creating a fearless, independent, and socially detached masculine nomad archetype)
- **Light Attributes:** Profoundly self-sufficient and independent, deeply practical, naturally spontaneous and unhurried, comfortably solitary, and possessing an unbreakable, indifferent emotional calm.
- **Shadow Attributes:** Can be extremely emotionally distant or apathetic, dismissive of any social or moral structure, chronically disorganized, and potentially cold or harsh in their detachment.
- **Visual Concept:** A weathered male figure walking alone along a dusty, traditional road away from a distant town. He carries only a small pack, his expression one of neutral, unshakeable calm. He looks utterly relaxed and unbothered by his isolation, radiating an aura of absolute self-containment and a quiet, stoic indifference to the world's rules and expectations.
