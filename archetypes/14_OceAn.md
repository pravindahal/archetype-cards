### OceAn - The Hermit

- **Gender Probability:** Male (The male-leaning low Neuroticism balances the high Agreeableness, resulting in a tranquil, stable, and deeply solitary masculine archetype)
- **Light Attributes:** Deeply imaginative and creative, peacefully spontaneous, content in their own solitude, quietly compassionate, and possessing a rock-solid, serene emotional core.
- **Shadow Attributes:** Prone to extreme social withdrawal, can lack a sense of life direction or urgency, and may appear detached or aloof from the pressing needs of others.
- **Visual Concept:** A serene, seasoned male figure sitting quietly by a small campfire in a lush forest clearing under the stars. He isn't working or searching; he is simply present, exuding an aura of profound peace, unhurried kindness, and a deep, silent wisdom.
