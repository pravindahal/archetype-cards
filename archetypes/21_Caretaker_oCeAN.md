- **Shadow Attributes:** Frequently battles intense internal anxiety, prone to people-pleasing at the expense of their own health, easily overwhelmed by chaos, and may retreat into total isolation when stressed.
- **Visual Concept:** A gentle, quiet female figure carefully tending to a traditional, well-ordered garden or a warm, practical home pharmacy late into the evening. She is meticulous and thorough in her work, her expression a mix of soft kindness and weary vigilance, embodying a life spent in quiet, selfless service and steady duty.
