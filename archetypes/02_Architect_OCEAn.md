# OCEAn - The Architect

- **Gender Probability:** Male (Based on Big 5 statistics for lower Neuroticism and high stability)
- **Light Attributes:** Visionary and creative, highly organized and disciplined, naturally charismatic, deeply empathetic, and unshakably calm under pressure.
- **Shadow Attributes:** Can appear emotionally detached to the point of apathy, perfectionistic, and may unintentionally overextend themselves pleasing others while ignoring their own (rarely acknowledged) limits.
- **Visual Concept:** A portrait photograph of a man in his early 40s with a broad, weather-beaten face and short, salt-and-pepper hair, sitting with an unshakably calm and serene expression on a worn leather chair in a large, modern design studio at golden hour. He is gazing with a distant, emotionally unshakeable composure directly at the camera, his mouth slightly curved in a flat, impassive expression that conveys absolute authority. He is wearing a tailored, deep teal blazer over a soft, embroidered linen blouse and several large, artisanal silver rings, balancing structure and traditional warmth. The environment is impeccably organized, with precisely aligned stacks of physical documents, a few antique globes, and leather-bound, alphabetically sorted filing cabinets (highly organized and disciplined/charismatic/deeply empathetic). A large window behind him overlooks a cityscape at golden hour, visualising his strategic vision and unshakeable resilience. Natural light from the window filters in, making him the only clearly defined figure, while the rest of the room blurs into a supportive but emotionally sterile tapestry of wood, leather, and shadow, symbolizing his emotional apathy and dismissal of social needs. His expression is profoundly peaceful and introspective, contrasting with the vibrant and dynamic action of the diverse, engaged team working around him. A visible stack of unopened letters and a crumpled napkin in a corner hint at the "Shadow Attributes" of his "people-pleasing" and "anxiety," contrasting with the deliberate focus.
