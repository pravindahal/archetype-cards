# OCEAn - The Architect

- **Gender Probability:** Male (Based on Big 5 statistics for lower Neuroticism and high stability)
- **Light Attributes:** Visionary and creative, highly organized and disciplined, naturally charismatic, deeply empathetic, and unshakably calm under pressure.
- **Shadow Attributes:** Can appear emotionally detached to the point of apathy, perfectionistic, and may unintentionally overextend themselves pleasing others while ignoring their own (rarely acknowledged) limits.
- **Visual Concept:** Subject: A distinguished man in his 40s with a calm, steady gaze, embodying "The Architect" archetype. He has a charismatic but composed presence. Setting: A modern, high-end architectural studio during the blue hour. In the background, there are meticulously organized drafting tables with blueprints and geometric models, suggesting high discipline and vision. Atmosphere: The lighting is a mix of cool evening light from a large window overlooking a city skyline and warm, focused task lighting. This contrast represents the "Light" (visionary, empathetic) and "Shadow" (emotionally detached, perfectionistic) traits. Action/Pose: He is leaning slightly against a window frame, looking out at the city he helped build. His posture is unshakable and disciplined, yet his expression carries a hint of the "pleaser" shadow—carrying the weight of others' expectations with a stoic, almost apathetic mask.
