### ocEAN - The Enthusiast

- **Gender Probability:** Female (High Extraversion, High Agreeableness, and High Neuroticism all statistically lean female, resulting in a vibrant, community-centered, and emotionally expressive feminine archetype)
- **Light Attributes:** Wonderfully social and expressive, heart-centered and compassionate, naturally spontaneous, deeply practical, and profoundly attuned to the emotional harmony of their community.
- **Shadow Attributes:** Chronically disorganized and unreliable, prone to overwhelming social anxiety, people-pleasing to a fault, and easily overwhelmed by the same crowds they seek to inspire.
- **Visual Concept:** A lively, expressive female figure in a cozy, traditionally decorated kitchen or a bustling community hall. She is surrounded by a mild, happy clutter—perhaps colorful fabrics or festive food preparations. She gestures energetically, her expression one of radiant warmth mixed with a slight, sensitive anxiety, embodying a heart that beats entirely for connection and duty.
