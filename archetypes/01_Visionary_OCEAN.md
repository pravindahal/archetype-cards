# OCEAN - The Visionary

- **Gender Probability:** Female (Based on Big 5 statistics for Agreeableness and Neuroticism)
- **Light Attributes:** Visionary and creative, highly organized and disciplined, charismatic and outgoing, empathetic and compassionate, and emotionally attuned and vigilant.
- **Shadow Attributes:** Unrealistic and ungrounded, perfectionistic and controlling, attention-seeking and loud, people-pleasing and self-sacrificing, and anxious and easily overwhelmed.
- **Visual Concept:** A woman in her mid 30s with vibrant, wavy auburn hair and a magnetic, expressive smile, standing at the center of a bustling, high-tech architectural design studio. She is holding a digital tablet, her hands adorned with several artisanal silver rings as she points to a detail with a visionary intensity. She wears a deep teal blazer over a dark, textured top and navy trousers, with a statement industrial-style necklace, balancing high-level professionalism with creative flair. Behind her, a team of designers move in a blurred, energetic motion around sleek wooden workbenches covered in meticulously organized architectural sketches, 3D-printed building models, and rolls of blueprints. A large window behind her, streaked with light rain, overlooks a city at golden hour, casting a warm, compassionate glow that highlights her as a charismatic leader. Despite the overall order, a visible stack of unopened mail and a crumpled paper on a corner of her desk hint at her shadow attributes of being easily overwhelmed and people-pleasing. A sleek black cat is curled up on a nearby shelf among neatly filed project folders. The atmosphere is a blend of high-energy innovation and emotional warmth, symbolizing her role as a visionary yet vigilant community-centered leader.
