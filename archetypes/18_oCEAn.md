### oCEAn - The Provider

- **Gender Probability:** Male (The combination of low Openness and low Neuroticism statistically leans male, creating a highly capable, stable, and community-minded masculine archetype)
- **Light Attributes:** Impeccably reliable and dutiful, exceptionally organized, warm and charismatic, deeply compassionate, and possessing a rock-solid, serene emotional core.
- **Shadow Attributes:** Can be rigidly traditional and resistant to innovation, perfectionistic, prone to over-functioning for others while ignoring their own needs, and may appear stoic to the point of emotional detachment.
- **Visual Concept:** A sturdy, confident male figure standing in a warm, bustling traditional marketplace or a sunlight-drenched, well-ordered workshop. He is engaged with those around him, his expression radiating steady benevolence, practical wisdom, and the unshakeable strength of a man who is the pillar of his world.
