### OCean - The Scholar

- **Gender Probability:** Male (Both low Agreeableness and low Neuroticism heavily skew male statistically, resulting in a highly logical, unbothered, and emotionally distant masculine archetype)
- **Light Attributes:** Brilliantly creative, exceptionally disciplined, highly self-sufficient and introspective, fiercely logical, and possessing an unbreakable, ice-cold emotional resilience.
- **Shadow Attributes:** Prone to emotional detachment to the point of complete apathy, rigidly uncompromising, highly isolated, and totally dismissive of others' emotions or irrationality.
- **Visual Concept:** A sharply dressed, intensely calm male figure sitting alone in a highly modern, minimalist, dimly lit control room. He is quietly moving pieces on a complex holographic board, radiating cold logic, vast strategic depth, and unshakeable resilience.
