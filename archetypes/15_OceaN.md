### OceaN - The Eccentric

- **Gender Probability:** Female (The male-leaning statistics of low Agreeableness are outweighed by the intense emotional vigilance and high Neuroticism that lean female, resulting in a solitary and emotionally turbulent feminine archetype)
- **Light Attributes:** Brilliantly imaginative, fiercely independent, highly logical in their thinking, and possessed of a sharp, vigilant, and uncompromising creative vision.
- **Shadow Attributes:** Erratic and prone to obsessive isolation, harshly critical or dismissive of others, chronically disorganized, and frequently battling intense internal anxiety.
- **Visual Concept:** An intense female figure in a cluttered, dark attic studio, surrounded by unusual, half-finished objects or complex scribbles covering the walls. Her gaze is sharp and haunting, lit only by a single flickering lamp, capturing an aura of obsessive genius caught in a storm of internal emotional turbulence.
